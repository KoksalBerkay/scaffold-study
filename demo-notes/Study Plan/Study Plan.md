## How to use the time and the tutor
Use two sessions each study day: normally 90 minutes each, or 45 minutes each on Saturday. A long question can continue across the session break; its assignment remains one whole question.

Start each paper in printed question order. Finish the newest paper before starting the older one. Revisit completed papers for retrieval practice.

Ask the tutor to explain prerequisites, work through the assigned question, and check your reasoning. Teaching and checking are included in study estimates. Use the eigenvalues slides for June Exercise 2, SQL exercises after July Q4, and Gauss slides during Physics Problem 1.

Scheduled minutes use the upper end of the corresponding estimate in [[Time Estimates]]. These are allowances, not measured completion times. Do not stretch a finished question to fill its allowance. Use the remaining time to solve it again independently.

Read the full question before starting. Several inventory excerpts are truncated. If the actual question changes the workload, log it and revise the estimate.

If a task overruns, use Saturday's spare capacity first. If necessary, displace Saturday's planned question and preserve paper order. Do not compress prerequisite teaching or exceed three hours.
